Rewards Tracker · Icon pitch · Round 2

The stripe becomes the progress bar

Your brief back to the studio: keep the card, make the spark separate from the card, and swap the abstract ring for something the app actually has — so the magnetic stripe is now a linear progress bar, partially filled toward the next reward. Every variation uses a warm gold spark (#F5B942) so it reads against both the white card and the crimson field. Four takes, labelled E–H to keep them distinct from round one.

Level Gold Ledger

Straight-on · calm

A perfectly level white card, stripe recast as a chunky gold progress bar at ~67% with a faint blush track, chip knockout below for card identity, gold spark floating clear of the top-right corner. The spark and the earned progress share one gold — they read as a single "value" system.

For: calmest and most iconic; gold-on-white progress reads instantly. Against: the most reserved of the four — less kinetic energy than the tilted takes.
