Concave Terminal

The polish pass

H exactly as you know it, with the craft tightened: the spark redrawn with concave curved edges so it flares like a glint rather than a diamond, slab corners softened to a friendlier radius, and the stripe dropped below the slab's midline so the spark's upward flare balances the composition.

For: the safest evolution — same idea, better drawn. Against: stripe sits low; the asymmetry is deliberate but worth a second look at wallpaper scale.

H2

Gilded Slab

The inversion

The palette flipped: a crimson card on a warm near-white field, with the progress fill now gold — so the fill and the spark merge into one continuous "gold value" gesture streaking across the card. The card becomes the brand object itself.

For: most distinctive on a light home screen; fill + spark reading as one gold stroke is a strong story. Against: light field is rarer for app icons (blends into light backgrounds); the merged spark reads slightly comet-like — a feature or a bug depending on taste.

Night Terminal Spark

The fusionStudio recommendation

H's composition in G's materials: charcoal card on the crimson field, the stripe filled with the crimson-to-ember gradient, and the gold concave spark landing exactly at the terminal — where it glows against the dark card. Signature panel and halo cut; five shapes, one gradient.

For: your favourite idea plus the premium dark materials; the gold-on-charcoal terminal is the strongest single moment in any concept so far, and it holds beautifully at 16px. Against: hard to name one — if pressed, dark cards photograph less cheerfully in App Store screenshots.

G2

Corner Break

The studio pick, refined

G with its known issues fixed: the spark redrawn crisp and centred exactly on the card's top-right corner so it breaks the silhouette across charcoal and crimson, the heavy glow halo removed (the gradient reads cleaner alone), the card recentred, and the stripe grown slightly for 16px legibility. Signature panel kept — it sells "back of card" at large sizes and vanishes politely below 32px.

For: the most distinctive outline of any concept — the corner-break spark makes it recognisable even in a dock of red icons. Against: the spark isn't tied to the progress metaphor as tightly as H3's terminal spark.
